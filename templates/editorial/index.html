(06) — Questions

Good to know.

Still curious? Email hello@example.com.

When do you roast?

Every Tuesday. Orders placed by Monday at midnight ship from that week's roast, so your beans arrive within 72 hours of roasting.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longest, but we'll grind for espresso, filter, French press or moka pot at no extra cost — just choose at checkout.

How does the subscription work?

Pick a coffee and a frequency, and save 15% on every bag. Skip, swap or cancel anytime from your account — no fees, no lock-in.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes. Shipping is free on orders over $40 and most orders arrive in 2–3 business days.
