Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Yes — any pizza can be made on our gluten-free crust (+$3), and we offer house-made cashew mozzarella for vegan pies.

How far do you deliver?

We deliver within 5 miles of the shop. Delivery is free on orders over $25.

Do you cater parties and events?

We do — from office lunches to weddings, with our mobile wood-fired oven. Use the catering form and we'll reply within a few hours.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Absolutely. Order online, pick a time, and your pizza will be hot and boxed when you arrive.
